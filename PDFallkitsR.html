🖨️ PDF → Searchable (OCR)
Scanned ya image-based PDF se bhi text nikaalo — har page ko image banake offline OCR (Tesseract.js) chalata hai. Garbled "?" wale text ka yeh sabse reliable fallback hai.
🖨️
PDF load karo (scanned ya image-based)

⏱️ OCR har page par time leta hai (~5-20 sec/page, scale jitna zyada utna slow). Bade PDF mein sirf zaroori pages ka range daalo to fast hoga. Higher scale + B&W preprocessing se accuracy kaafi better hoti hai, especially chhote/blurry text par.
